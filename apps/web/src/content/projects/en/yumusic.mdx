## About YuMusic (Desktop)

The desktop sibling of the Garmin YuMusic app. Connects to any Subsonic-compatible server (Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic) and gives you a clean, dark desktop player for your self-hosted library. No streaming service required — your music stays where it already lives.

### Features

- Browse artists, albums, songs, playlists from your own server
- Dark, minimal UI designed for long listening sessions
- Streaming over your LAN or VPN — no third-party uploads
- Gapless playback and offline caching for selected albums
- Cross-platform Electron build (Linux, Windows, macOS)

### Why not just use $OTHER_PLAYER?

Most desktop Subsonic clients feel like web wrappers with extra clutter. YuMusic is purpose-built — a single-purpose player that gets out of the way and lets you listen.

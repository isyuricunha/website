## About LinkedIn Mass Unfollow

LinkedIn makes it painful to clean up your network — clicking "unfollow" one by one through thousands of connections isn't viable. This tool gives you two browser-script modes: a safe **soft mode** that only touches the connections visible on the current page, and a **hard mode** that auto-scrolls and processes the full list.

### Features

- **Soft mode** — unfollow only what's rendered (safe for audits)
- **Hard mode** — auto-scrolls through the full connection list
- **Progress tracking** with live counters and session summaries
- **Configurable delays and limits** to stay clear of LinkedIn's rate limits
- **Pause / resume / stop** at any moment
- **Detailed timestamped logs** so you know exactly what happened

### Safety

Built-in caps (50 in soft mode, 500 in hard mode) keep you from burning through your network by accident, and default delays are deliberately conservative to avoid flagging your account. Use responsibly.

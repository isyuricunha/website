## About YuMusic

YuMusic transforms your Garmin watch into a standalone offline music player for your personal **Subsonic-compatible** media server — Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic, or Nextcloud Music. Once connected via the Garmin Connect phone app, you can browse your playlists directly on your watch, download tracks over Wi-Fi, and leave your phone at home.

### Key Features

- **Direct server sync** — download playlists straight from your home server to your wrist
- **Offline playback** — listen anywhere, even without internet
- **Fluid native UI** — fast layout focused on getting you to your music without clutter
- **Shuffle All** across the full downloaded library
- **Auto-tracking offline scrobbles** synced back to your server
- **Audio quality control** (320 / 192 / 128 / 96 kbps) to fit your watch storage
- **Compact multi-playlist library** — shared tracks stored once, not per-playlist
- **Safe playlist removal** without breaking other playlists

### Stack

Built in Monkey C using Garmin's Connect IQ SDK, talking to the Subsonic API. Tested against Navidrome and Gonic; compatible with most Subsonic-API implementations.
